AUDIO IN / ANALOG OUT

Make analog damage move to the music.

reactive-vcr maps bass, frequency bands, and transients to more than sixty ntsc-rs controls and every bender-rs circuit bend—frame by frame, from a readable TOML file. Tune the look in the desktop app and render it, or take it on air with your camera, your mic, and OBS.

PATCH BAY

Three inputs. One reactive signal chain.

  1. 01

    Bring video

    Use any source GStreamer can decode.

  2. 02

    Bring audio

    Drive the effect with a separate track or the video's own sound.

  3. 03

    Patch parameters

    Map sound features to ntsc-rs and bender-rs controls in the desktop app or in TOML.

reactive-vcr render --video in.mp4 --audio song.wav --map fx.toml -o out.mp4

MONITOR OUT

Hear it. See it. Tweak it live.

  1. 01

    Drop in a video and a song

    The preview applies the effect to every frame in sync with the sound.

  2. 02

    Watch the meters

    Per-frame meters show the audio features and every modulated setting.

  3. 03

    Save and render

    Write the mapping as TOML, then queue H.264 MP4 or lossless FFV1 MKV renders.

reactive-vcr play --video in.mp4 --audio song.wav --map fx.toml

ON AIR

Put the damage on your stream.

  1. 01

    Point a camera at the room

    Your webcam runs through the deck in real time while your microphone drives it. The tape tears on the kick and snows when the crowd gets loud.

  2. 02

    Add it to any OBS scene

    Drop the reactive-vcr filter on a webcam, game capture, or screen share, then pick the mic, music bed, or desktop audio that drives it.

  3. 03

    Change looks mid-show

    Save the mapping and the new treatment lands within a quarter second, and the stream keeps going.

reactive-vcr stream --map fx.toml

GET THE DECK

An app, a binary, an OBS plugin, and a mapping file.

On Apple Silicon macOS, download reactive-vcr.app with GStreamer bundled inside and the command-line tool included. On x86_64 or arm64 Linux, the archive holds reactive-vcr and reactive-vcr-gui, which use the system GStreamer. No FFmpeg required. Streaming? Grab the OBS plugin for Windows, macOS, or Linux and drop it in your OBS plugins folder.
